Selective Prediction for Segmentation

Interpretable AI Inference Uncertainty

Overview

MSc Dissertation: a model-agnostic selective prediction framework that determines when an AI segmentation model is clinically safe to use or should defer to a radiologist. The goal is to bridge the gap between AI capability and clinical trust.

Technical Approach

Monte Carlo Dropout (MCD) and Test-Time Augmentation (TTA) estimate voxel-level uncertainty, which is aggregated via entropy and mutual information into per-scan deferral decisions.

The evaluation pipeline produces accuracy-coverage curves, calibration plots, and uncertainty heatmaps, providing clinicians with interpretable metrics for understanding model confidence.
