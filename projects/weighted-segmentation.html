Hierarchically Weighted Image Segmentation

3D Res-UNet Hierarchical loss IMRT

Overview

Group project: designed a hierarchical loss function encoding radiosensitivity and anatomical proximity for prostate IMRT (Intensity-Modulated Radiation Therapy) planning. The model was trained on 589 multi-institutional MRI volumes.

Results

Achieved 0.886 mean foreground Dice Similarity Coefficient. Improved neurovascular bundle (NVB) segmentation from 0.630 to 0.659 over the unweighted baseline, a clinically significant improvement for treatment planning.

Conducted ablation studies across 3 loss functions and 3 weighting schemes to validate the hierarchical approach and identify the optimal configuration.
