Sokoban Procedural Puzzle Generator

Two-Archive EA JSoko solver PSPACE-Complete

Overview

BSc Dissertation: a Java procedural content generation tool for PSPACE-Complete Sokoban puzzles. The generator works backwards from goal states to starting states, ensuring every generated puzzle is solvable.

Technical Approach

Combines stochastic generation with a Two-Archive evolutionary algorithm. The dual-archive approach maintains separate populations for quality and novelty, producing diverse and challenging puzzles.

All generated puzzles are validated against the JSoko optimised solver. Evaluation showed the generated puzzles outperformed hand-crafted baseline puzzles in complexity metrics. Tableau was used for data visualisation and analysis of puzzle characteristics.
